# Beyond.Praxis — Master Architecture Document
## "Beyond the white coat — tools for the modern medical mind"
### Built by Jasir Sajidh | Part of Ayuzee Platform

---

## Vision

Beyond.Praxis is the first "Life Operating System" for medical professionals.
While every other platform focuses on clinical skills or patient management,
Beyond.Praxis focuses on the PERSON behind the stethoscope — their growth,
their wealth, their time, their leadership, their life balance, and their legacy.

---

## Target Audience

| Persona | Age | Pain Points |
|---------|-----|-------------|
| Medical Student (MBBS/BAMS/BDS) | 18-24 | Exam stress, career confusion, zero financial literacy, burnout |
| PG Resident / Junior Doctor | 24-30 | 80-hr weeks, no time beyond medicine, isolation, imposter syndrome |
| Senior / Busy Practitioner | 30-50 | Wants to teach/write/invest/lead but no time or platform |
| Academic / HOD | 40-60 | Legacy building, mentoring next generation, thought leadership |

## Platform Modules (Combined Architecture)

### MODULE 1: Wheel of Life (Core — Everything Connects Here)

The Wheel is the heartbeat of Beyond.Praxis. Every other module feeds into it.

**8 Spokes (Doctor-Adapted):**

| # | Spoke | What it Measures | Example Low-Score Trigger |
|---|-------|-----------------|--------------------------|
| 1 | Clinical Excellence | Skills, knowledge, patient outcomes | → Suggest: Case studies, CME tracker |
| 2 | Finance & Wealth | Savings, investments, debt, tax | → Suggest: Finance toolkit pathway |
| 3 | Time & Productivity | Control over schedule, deep work hours | → Suggest: Time management module |
| 4 | Leadership & Influence | Team leading, teaching, public speaking | → Suggest: Leadership skills lab |
| 5 | Relationships & Social | Partner, friends, networking quality | → Suggest: Communication pathway |
| 6 | Family & Presence | Time with kids/parents, being present | → Suggest: Boundary-setting guide |
| 7 | Health & Wellness | Sleep, exercise, stress, burnout score | → Suggest: Wellness hub |
| 8 | Joy, Hobbies & Growth | Creative pursuits, learning, fun | → Suggest: Side-income or hobby guide |

**Features:**
- Monthly self-assessment (rate each spoke 1-10)
- Interactive radar/spider chart visualization
- Historical trend tracking (3/6/12 months)
- Imbalance alerts ("Your Wellness dropped 3 points")
- AI-guided nudges based on weak spokes
- Goal setting per spoke per quarter
- Accountability partner pairing
- Quarterly "Life Design" guided workshop
- Exportable progress report (PDF)

**Guidance Books for Wheel of Life:**

### MODULE 2: Time Management Toolkit

**Why:** Medical professionals have the most fragmented schedules of any profession.
Generic time-management apps don't understand 36-hr shifts, on-call rotations, or
5-minute gaps between patients.

**Features:**

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Doctor's Time Audit | Log a week → visual breakdown of time leaks |
| Eisenhower Matrix (Medical) | Pre-categorized: Urgent-Patient / Important-Growth / Admin-Noise / Eliminate |
| Pomodoro for Doctors | 25-min focus blocks + "between patients" micro-task mode (5 min) |
| Weekly Planning Ritual | Sunday 15-min guided session: clinical, study, personal, rest allocation |
| Time-Block Templates | Pre-built for: PG resident, private practitioner, academic doctor, student |
| Energy Mapping | Track high/low energy hours → auto-suggest when to do deep work |
| Stolen Time Finder | Identify 5-min gaps → suggest micro-learning or wellness fills |
| Monthly Time Review | "You spent 12 hours on admin this month — here's how to reduce it" |
| Calendar Integration | Sync with Google Calendar, export time blocks |

### MODULE 4: Guided Self-Development Pathways

**Why:** Doctors don't have time to "figure out what to learn next." Give them
structured, opinionated pathways with clear outcomes and daily actions.

**Pathway Structure:**
```
Pathway (4-8 weeks)
  └── Module (5-7 per pathway)
        └── Lesson (5-min read/watch)
              ├── Key Insight
              ├── Action Item ("Do this today")
              ├── Reflection Question
              ├── Book/Podcast Recommendation
              └── Community Discussion Thread
```

**Pre-built Pathways:**

| # | Pathway | Duration | Outcome |
|---|---------|----------|---------|

| 8 | Mindful Doctor | 4 weeks | Daily mindfulness practice |
| 9 | Clinic Setup Masterclass | 8 weeks | Location, financials, branding done |
| 10 | Effective Communication | 4 weeks | Handle any difficult conversation |
| 11 | Work-Life Integration | 4 weeks | Boundaries set, family time protected |

**Features:**
- Daily micro-actions (takes 5-15 min)
- Progress tracking with streaks
- Completion certificates (shareable)

## How Everything Connects (The Intelligence Layer)

```
                    ┌─────────────────────────┐
                    │    WHEEL OF LIFE         │
                    │   (Central Dashboard)    │
                    └────────────┬────────────┘
                                 │
              Score < 5 on any spoke triggers:
                                 │
         ┌───────────┬───────────┼───────────┬───────────┐
         ▼           ▼           ▼           ▼           ▼
   ┌──────────┐ ┌──────────┐ ┌──────────┐ ┌──────────┐ ┌──────────┐
   │  TIME    │ │LEADERSHIP│ │ FINANCE  │ │ WELLNESS │ │  CAREER  │
   │  MGMT   │ │  SKILLS  │ │ TOOLKIT  │ │   HUB    │ │NAVIGATOR │
   └────┬─────┘ └────┬─────┘ └────┬─────┘ └────┬─────┘ └────┬─────┘
        │             │            │             │             │
        └─────────────┼────────────┼─────────────┼─────────────┘
                      ▼            ▼             ▼
              ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
              │        GUIDED PATHWAYS                     │
              │  (Structured 4-8 week programs)           │
              └──────────────────┬───────────────────────┘
                                 │
                      ┌──────────┼──────────┐
                      ▼          ▼          ▼
              ┌────────────┐ ┌────────┐ ┌──────────────┐
              │MICRO-LEARN │ │ BOOKS  │ │ REFLECTIONS  │
              │  (5 min)   │ │LIBRARY │ │  & JOURNAL   │
              └────────────┘ └────────┘ └──────────────┘
```

**Connection Rules:**
1. Wheel of Life assessment drives ALL recommendations
2. Low Time spoke → Time Management module activates + relevant pathway
3. Low Leadership spoke → Leadership Lab unlocks challenges
4. Low Finance spoke → Finance Toolkit highlights + "Financial Freedom" pathway
5. Books are recommended contextually (never generic "read this" — always "because your X score is low")
6. Pathways reference specific tools in other modules
7. Micro-learning fills time gaps identified by Time Management module
8. Weekly reflection journal connects back to Wheel reassessment

## Philosophy: Tools, Not Pages

Beyond.Praxis is NOT a content website. It's a **toolbox**. Every module
delivers an interactive tool that the user uses repeatedly. Content (lessons,
books, guides) exists only to support the tool usage.

**The difference:**
- Content site: "Here's an article about time management" → user reads once, leaves
- Tool-based: "Here's YOUR time audit dashboard" → user returns daily to log, track, improve
