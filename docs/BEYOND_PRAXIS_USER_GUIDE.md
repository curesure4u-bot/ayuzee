## 1. What is Beyond.Praxis?

Beyond.Praxis is a **Life Operating System for Medical Professionals**. It helps doctors, students, and healthcare workers grow across 8 dimensions of life — not just clinical skills.

**The 8 Spokes (Wheel of Life):**
| Spoke | What It Covers |
|-------|---------------|
| Clinical Excellence | Skills, knowledge, patient outcomes, CME |
| Finance & Wealth | Savings, investments, budgeting, tax |
| Time & Productivity | Schedule control, deep work, Pomodoro |
| Leadership & Influence | Team leading, teaching, public speaking |
| Relationships & Social | Friends, networking, community |
| Family & Presence | Kids, parents, being present |
| Health & Wellness | Sleep, exercise, stress, burnout |
| Joy & Hobbies | Creativity, travel, fun, hobbies |

**Key Principle:** Every module maps to a spoke. The dashboard shows how ALL modules connect and feed into your overall life balance.

## 3. The Dashboard — Your Command Center

**URL:** `ayuzee.com/beyond`

The dashboard is **interconnected** — it pulls real-time data from ALL modules and shows:

### What You See:

| Section | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Greeting + Today's Focus** | Personalized message + your #1 priority for today |
| **XP Progress Bar** | Your level, XP, and progress to next level |
| **Today's Priorities** | Urgent items: streak risks, deadlines, weak spokes |
| **Smart Recommendations** | AI-generated actions based on your weakest spokes |
| **Spoke Health Grid** | All 8 spokes with combined scores (self-assessment + actual activity) |
| **Quick Tools** | One-tap access to all modules |
| **Active Challenges** | Current challenges you can complete |
| **Radar Chart** | Visual of your wheel (purple = self-score, green = activity) |
| **Stats** | Total XP, badges, health score, active modules |
| **Active Goals** | Your spoke goals with progress bars |
| **Recent Activity** | Live feed of XP earned across all modules |
| **Motivational Insight** | Daily inspiration quote |

### How Scores Work:

```
Combined Score = (Wheel Self-Score × 60%) + (Activity Score × 40%)
```

- **Self-Score:** What you rate yourself (1-10) in Wheel of Life
- **Activity Score:** Computed from your actual actions in modules (0-10)
- This means: you can't just SAY you're good at something — you have to DO it

## 4. Wheel of Life — Core Assessment

**URL:** `/beyond/wheel-of-life`  
**Frequency:** Monthly (reminder appears at 25+ days)  
**XP Reward:** +100 XP + 25 Coins per assessment

### How to Use:

1. Rate each of the 8 spokes from 1-10 honestly
2. Add optional notes about what's changed
3. Click "Save Assessment"
4. View your history and trends in the "History" tab
5. Check "Insights" tab for automatic recommendations

### SOP:

- Take assessment at the **same time each month** (1st of month recommended)
- Be **honest** — inflated scores give you bad recommendations
- Write notes about **why** a score changed
- Review the "weak spokes" suggestions after saving
- Set at least one goal per weak spoke

## 6. Gamification System

### XP (Experience Points)

| Action | XP Earned |
|--------|-----------|
| Wheel of Life assessment | +100 |
| Complete a Pomodoro session | +50 |
| Finish a book | +75 |
| Leadership scenario | +100 |
| Breathing exercise | +25 |
| Mood log | +25 |
| Journal entry | +50 |
| Habit check-off (daily) | +5-10 |
| Life Planner task done | +5 |
| Community post | +50 |
| Pathway lesson | +50-100 |
| Challenge completion | +50-200 |

### Levels

| Level | Title | XP Required |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Intern | 0 |
| 2 | Junior Resident | 500 |
| 3 | Senior Resident | 1,500 |
| 4 | Registrar | 3,500 |
| 5 | Consultant | 7,000 |
| 6 | Associate Professor | 12,000 |
| 7 | Professor | 20,000 |
| 8 | Department Head | 35,000 |
| 9 | Dean | 55,000 |
| 10 | Praxis Master | 80,000 |

### Streaks

7 streak types tracked: Login, Learning, Wellness, Planning, Reading, Reflection, Finance

**Milestones:** 7 days, 14 days, 30 days, 60 days, 100 days — each gives bonus XP + coins

**IMPORTANT:** Streaks break after 48 hours of inactivity. "At risk" warning shows at 36 hours.

### Coins

Earned alongside XP. Can be spent in the coin store for rewards.

### Badges

Earned automatically when criteria are met. Examples:
- "Mirror Mirror" — First Wheel assessment
- "Balanced Life" — All spokes >= 6
- "Marathon Mind" — 30-day learning streak
- "Centurion" — 100-day streak of any type

## 8. Do's and Don'ts

### DO's

| # | Do This | Why |
|---|---------|-----|
| 1 | Log in every day | Maintains login streak + daily briefing updates |
| 2 | Take Wheel assessment monthly | Keeps recommendations accurate |
| 3 | Start with "Today's Priorities" | They're sorted by urgency for a reason |
| 4 | Follow Smart Recommendations | They target your weakest areas specifically |
| 5 | Keep at least 3 streaks active | Builds compound growth habits |
| 6 | Log mood daily | Most impactful 30-second action for self-awareness |
| 7 | Complete at least 1 challenge/week | Keeps engagement + XP flowing |
| 8 | Post in community weekly | Builds social spoke + helps others |
| 9 | Plan tomorrow the night before | Single biggest productivity lever |
| 10 | Be honest in self-assessments | Fake scores = bad recommendations |
| 11 | Use the "Apply It" feature for books | Locks in knowledge transfer |
| 12 | Set goals per spoke in Wheel | Goals with deadlines get done |
| 13 | Review your radar chart weekly | Visual keeps you honest about balance |
| 14 | Use Pomodoro for deep work | 25min focus beats 2 hours of distraction |
| 15 | Celebrate badge unlocks | Positive reinforcement matters |

### DON'Ts

| # | Don't Do This | Why |
|---|---------------|-----|
| 1 | Don't inflate Wheel scores | You'll get wrong recommendations, miss real growth areas |
| 2 | Don't try to do ALL modules daily | Start with 3-4, add more as habits form |
| 3 | Don't ignore "at risk" streak warnings | Losing a 30-day streak hurts motivation |
| 4 | Don't skip the Wheel assessment | Everything else depends on accurate spoke scores |
| 5 | Don't just read books — Apply them | Reading without application is entertainment, not growth |
| 6 | Don't compare leaderboard obsessively | It's for motivation, not competition |
| 7 | Don't create too many habits at once | 3-5 max. Master those, then add more |
| 8 | Don't use the platform only for XP | XP is a side-effect of real growth actions |
| 9 | Don't ignore the "Family" spoke | Most doctors neglect this — the app reminds you |
| 10 | Don't share login credentials | Each user needs their own account for accurate tracking |
| 11 | Don't delete your Wheel history | Trends over time are the most valuable data |
| 12 | Don't admin-moderate without checking | Always read the full post before any admin action |
| 13 | Don't create challenges with impossible criteria | Users lose trust if challenges feel unfair |
| 14 | Don't neglect Joy spoke | Burnout prevention requires intentional fun |
| 15 | Don't check the app only when you "have time" | 2 minutes (mood log + habit check) is enough on busy days |

## 9. Daily Workflow (Recommended)

### Morning (2-3 minutes)

1. Open Beyond.Praxis dashboard
2. Read your **greeting + today's focus**
3. Check **Today's Priorities** — address any "high urgency" items
4. Glance at **Smart Recommendations** — pick 1 to do today

### During Work (as needed)

5. Start a **Pomodoro** before deep-focus tasks
6. Log **mood** at midday (30 seconds)
7. If stressed → do a **2-min breathing exercise**

### Evening (5 minutes)

8. Check off your **habits** for the day
9. Complete **Life Planner** tasks or add tomorrow's
10. Write a quick **gratitude entry** (3 items)
11. Optionally: journal entry or read 10 pages of a book
12. Check your **streak status** — make sure nothing is at risk

### Weekly (15 minutes, Sunday recommended)

13. Review your **Spoke Health Grid** — any dropped spokes?
14. Take on a new **weekly challenge**
15. Post something in **Community**
16. Review **Wheel radar chart** — is the shape balanced?
17. Complete 1-2 **Leadership scenarios**

### Monthly (20 minutes, 1st of month)

18. Take **Wheel of Life assessment**
19. Set/review **spoke goals**
20. Review **progress** — celebrate wins
21. Adjust approach for any chronically low spokes

## 10. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Can't log in | Check email/password. Use "Forgot Password" if needed. |
| Dashboard shows "Not authenticated" | Your session expired. Log out and log back in. |
| Wheel scores not updating | Make sure you click "Save Assessment" — don't just move sliders |
| Streak broke unexpectedly | Streaks require activity within 48 hours. Check timezone. |
| No recommendations showing | You need at least 1 Wheel assessment for recommendations to work |
| XP not appearing | Refresh the page. XP may take a few seconds to sync. |
| Can't see Hero Admin | Only authorized accounts have access. Contact Dr. Saleem. |
| Module page is empty | Some modules need seed data. Admin should add content. |
| Radar chart is blank | Take your first Wheel of Life assessment to populate it. |
